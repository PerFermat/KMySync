The app is now called KMySync - the name says what it does: your KMyMoney data, on your own server, recorded while you are out. The most important change is under the hood: an export can no longer damage your .kmy file. The app first writes it in full under a temporary name and only then has it put in place, so a dead spot or a timeout in the middle of a transfer can no longer half-overwrite your file. To match, "Test connection" now checks the whole chain up to the write, rename and delete permission in the target folder - for Nextcloud, WebDAV and SMB alike. While it runs, a band shows which step is in progress and how the last one turned out; if something is wrong, a report appears with the raw status code and what it means - such as the hint that two-factor authentication needs an app password here. The report can be copied and contains neither the password nor the user name. Also: an edited booking no longer loses its reconciliation when written back; learned portfolio rules can be shared and imported; securities documents are named by type and instrument; and a portfolio movement shows its note and its document.
