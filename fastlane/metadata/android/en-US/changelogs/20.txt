New: read and write encrypted KMyMoney files – the .kmy with GPG, the database with SQLCipher. The app warns when you import your private main key; the encryption subkey is enough.
New: a folder on the device as sync target, e.g. the folder of a sync app such as Syncthing.
Securities and accounts in a foreign currency are converted into the base currency using KMyMoney's exchange rates; the portfolio view shows the date of each price.
